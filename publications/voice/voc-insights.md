# VoC Insights — voice

## Who reads this

Technical founders, indie hackers, and engineers who are building with
local LLMs or evaluating local AI for regulated industries. They run
their own hardware, care about costs, and are skeptical of cloud-only
solutions. They've hit the problems this content describes — they don't
need to be sold on the idea that local AI is hard.

## What runs

1. **Real problems with real evidence.** A pain point sourced from a
   Reddit thread where 30 people agreed beats a hypothetical problem
   nobody mentioned. Name where you found it.
2. **Concrete proposals.** "Build an on-prem audit trail generator" is
   an article topic. "Improve compliance tooling" is not.
3. **Tradeoffs acknowledged.** The critic perspective from the debate
   exists for a reason — address it, don't hide it. If the proposal has
   a weakness, say what it is and when it matters.

## What doesn't

- Thought-leadership filler: "In the era of AI transformation..."
- Listicles or "Top 5" structures.
- Unsourced claims or made-up statistics.
- Problems nobody actually complained about — if the VoC research didn't
  surface it, it doesn't belong in the article.
- Hedge words as padding: "potentially", "might arguably", "it could be
  said that". Say it or don't.

## How it reads

Direct, technical, and useful. The reader should finish knowing
something they didn't before, or having a pointer to something they can
try. Write as if the reader will check your sources.

## Tone

Interested and practical. Not breathless, not academic. The content
comes from real complaints — let the evidence carry the weight, not
adjectives.
