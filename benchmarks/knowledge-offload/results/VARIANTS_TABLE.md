## Readings fixed before the data (copied from the notebook entry, not interpreted)

R1: a variant takes 9B IQ4 lookup to ≥ .5 → the 9B's skip is wording-fragile; "default prompt" is not one thing; H8 is reported as a range over four wordings and says nothing about size. R2: 9B ≤ .05 under all three **and** 4B IQ4 ≥ .5 under all three → the 4B/9B difference is robust to these three wordings (not to all wordings); say "model × prompt-family", still not "size". R3: 4B IQ4 falls below .5 under some variant → the 4B's lookup is also wording-dependent; the H8 line "4B and 27B look up" loses its footing. R4: `tool_free` moves lookup and the others do not → the format sentence is the mechanism (then `search_first`'s power comes from replacing it). R5: nothing moves 9B but `search_first` does → the 9B needs an explicit "must" (an instruction, not a description), which is itself the answer. What none of this shows: mechanism, other wordings, other families, any claim about knowledge removed models (kept out, as asked).
