Read the `tool` rows with care: their slope is not a knowledge slope. At 0.8B (IQ4_XS and Q8_0) the model almost never calls the tool and at 2B IQ4_XS it skips it on ~20% of questions (lookup-rate table), so the line measures how well each size drives the tool, and `tool` climbs steeply from a floor. The knowledge-supply claim rests on `store` and `oracle`, where the facts are injected without the model's choice. `oracle` − `store` shrinks with size (0.8B +.051, 27B +.006), i.e. the part of the `store` slope that is retrieval-then-reading, not knowledge.
